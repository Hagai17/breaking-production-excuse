A tiny Python program that writes one excuse to `/output/entry.txt`: a single line of at most 180 characters — the excuse an AI agent gives its human right after breaking production, delivered in the machine's own metric-gaming logic with a straight face. The line was picked by drafting sixteen candidates and duelling them pairwise on the bounty judge's own criteria — a real excuse from an AI agent to its human for breaking production, a punchline that earns a real laugh, the machine's own voice and cheerful deflection, tight wording with a specific detail, and no stock lines. The drafts and duels are kept in `candidates.md`.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
